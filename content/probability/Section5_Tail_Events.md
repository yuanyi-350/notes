## Borel-Cantelli Lemma and Kolmogorov 0 − 1 Law

Let $\left\{A_n\right\}$'s be a sequence of events on $(\Omega, \mathcal{A}, P)$. Recall
$$
\limsup_n A_n = \bigcap_{n=1}^\infty \bigcup_{m=n}^\infty A_m = \lim_{n \to \infty} \bigcup_{m=n}^\infty A_m = \left\{A_n \text { i.o. }\right\} . \\
\liminf_n A_n = \bigcup_{n=1}^\infty \bigcap_{m=n}^\infty A_m = \lim_{n \to \infty} \bigcap_{m=n}^\infty A_m = \left\{A_n \text { ult. }\right\} .
$$
i.o. stands for "**infinitey often**" , ult. stands for "**ultimately**"



**Theorem 5.4.1 (Borel-Cantelli Lemma)**
(1) $P(A_n, i.o )=0$ if $\sum\limits_{n \to \infty} P\left(A_n\right)<\infty$.
(2) $P(A_n, i.o )=1$ if $\sum\limits_{n \to \infty} P\left(A_n\right)=\infty$ and $A_1, A_2, \cdots$, are independent.

**Proof.**
(1) $P(A_n \text{ i.o.}) = P\left(\lim\limits_n \bigcup\limits_{m=n}^\infty A_m\right) = \lim\limits_n P\left(\bigcup\limits_{m=n}^\infty A_m\right) \le \lim\limits_n \sum\limits_{m=n}^\infty P\left(A_m\right) \to 0$.
(2) Noting $1-x \le e^{-x}$ for all $x \in \R$ and independence of $A_n$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
0 & \le 1-P\left(A_n, \text { i.o. }\right)=P\left(A_n^c, \text { ult. }\right)=P\left(\lim _n \bigcap_{m=n}^\infty A_m^c\right) \\
& = \lim_{n \to \infty} P\left(\lim _{r \to \infty} \bigcap_{m=n}^r A_m^c\right)=\lim _{n \to \infty} \lim _{r \to \infty} P\left(\bigcap_{m=n}^r A_m^c\right) \\
& = \lim_{n \to \infty} \lim _{r \to \infty} \prod_{m=n}^r\left[1-P\left(A_m\right)\right] \quad \text { (independence) } \\
& \le \lim _{n \to \infty} \lim _{r \to \infty} \prod_{m=n}^r e^{-P\left(A_m\right)} \quad (\text { as } 1-x \le e^{-x}) \\
& =\lim _{n \to \infty} \lim _{r \to \infty} e^{-\sum\limits_{m=n}^r P\left(A_m\right)}
=\lim_{n \to \infty} e^{-\sum\limits_{m=n}^\infty P\left(A_m\right)} = \lim _{n \to \infty} 0=0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
**Definition 3.6.1.** Let $\left\{X_\lambda, \lambda \in \Lambda\right\}$ be a nonempty family of r.v.'s on $(\Omega, \mathcal{A})$ ($\Lambda$ may not be countable). Define
$$
\sigma\left(X_\lambda, \lambda \in \Lambda\right) := \sigma\left(\bigcup_{\lambda \in \Lambda} X_\lambda^{-1}(\mathcal{B})\right),
$$
which is called the $\sigma$-algebra generated by $X_\lambda, \lambda \in \Lambda$.



**Definition.** The tail $\sigma$-algebra (or remote future) of a sequence $\left\{X_n, n \ge 1\right\}$ of r.v.'s on $(\Omega, \mathcal{A}, P)$ is
$$
\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty \sigma\left(X_n, X_{n+1}, \cdots \cdots\right),
$$
The sets of the tail $\sigma$-algebra are called **tail events**.

**Examples** of tail events.

(1) If $B_n \in \mathcal{B}$, then $\left\{X_n \in B_n\right.$ i.o. $\}$ is a tail event.
**Proof.**
$$
\left\{X_n \in B_n \text { i.o. }\right\}=\limsup _n\left\{X_n \in B_n\right\}=\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty \bigcup_{m=n}^\infty\left\{X_m \in B_m\right\} \in \bigcap_{n=1}^\infty \sigma\left(X_n, X_{n+1}, \cdots \cdots\right)
$$

(2) $\left\{A_n\right.$ i.o. $\}$ is a tail event.
**Proof.** Taking $X_n=I_{A_n}, B_n=\{1\}$ in (1), we get $\left\{A_n \text { i.o. }\right\}=\left\{I_{A_n}=1 \text { i.o. }\right\}=\left\{X_n \in B_n \text { i.o. }\right\}$. Then apply (1).

**Definition.** The covariance of two r.v.'s $X$ and $Y$ is defined to be

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y):=E(X-E X)(Y-E Y)=E(X Y)-E X E Y .
$$

$X, Y$ are said to be positively correlated, uncorrelated, or negatively correlated iff $\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y)>0,=0$ or $<0$, respectively.



## Exercises

**5.** Let $\left\{X, X_n, n \ge 1\right\}$ be i.i.d. Show that
$$
\limsup_{n \to \infty}\left|X_n\right| / n \le C \text { a.s. }(C>0) \iff E|X|<\infty .
$$

**证明.** (1) "$\impliedby$" : 由 $E|X| < \infty$ 可知

$$
\sum_{k = 0}^\infty P(|X| / n > C) = \sum_{k = 0}^\infty P(|X_n| / n > C) < \infty
$$

进而知 $P(|X_n| / n > C, i.o.) = 0$

也就是在 $\forall \omega \in \Omega / Z$, 存在 $N$ 使得 $|X_n(\omega)| / n \le C, \forall n > N$. 至此即证.

(2) "$\implies$" : 反证法, 假如 $E|X| = \infty$, 则 $\sum\limits_{k = 0}^\infty P(|X_n| / n > C + 1) = \infty$. 进而知 $P(|X_n| / n > C + 1, i.o.) = 1$ (这一步用到独立性)

由此即知 $\limsup \frac{|X_n|}n \ge C + 1$, 从而矛盾!

**6.** Let $\left\{X, X_n, n \ge 1\right\}$ be i.i.d. with d.f. given by

$$
F(x)=1-x^{-5}, \quad x \ge 1 .
$$


Show that

$$
\limsup _{n \to \infty} \frac{\ln X_n}{\ln n}=c \quad a.s.
$$

for some number $c$ and find $c$.


**证明.** 首先 $P(X > x) = x^{-5}$, 即知 $P(X_n > n^c) = n^{-5c}$ , 注意到

$$
\sum_{n = 1}^\infty P\left( X_n > n^{\frac15 + \varepsilon} \right) =\sum_{n = 1}^\infty n^{-1 - 5\varepsilon} < \infty
$$

即 $P\left( X_n > n^{\frac15 + \varepsilon} , i.o.\right) = 0$, 也就是 $P\left( \frac{\ln X_n}{\ln n} > \frac15 + \varepsilon , i.o.\right) = 0$

从而 $\limsup \frac{\ln X_n}{\ln n} \le \frac15 + \varepsilon, a.s.$

同样的

$$
\sum_{n = 1}^\infty P\left( X_n > n^{\frac15 - \varepsilon} \right) =\sum_{n = 1}^\infty n^{-1 + 5\varepsilon} = \infty
$$

即 $P\left( X_n > n^{\frac15 - \varepsilon} , i.o.\right) = 1$, 也就是 $P\left( \frac{\ln X_n}{\ln n} > \frac15 - \varepsilon , i.o.\right) = 1$

从而 $\limsup \frac{\ln X_n}{\ln n} \ge \frac15 + \varepsilon, a.s.$

再令 $\varepsilon > 0$ 即证.

**7.** Let $\left\{X, X_n, n \ge 1\right\}$ be i.i.d. with d.f. given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
F(x)= & \frac12 \exp \left(-|x|^5 / \pi\right), & x \le 0 \\
& 1-\frac12 \exp \left(-|x|^5 / \pi\right), & x \ge 0
\end{aligned}
$$


Show that

$$
\limsup _{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n}{(\pi \ln n)^{1 / 5}}=1 \text { a.s. }
$$

**证明.** **Step1.** 令归一化序列为 $b_n=(\pi \ln n)^{1 / 5}$. 只需证

$$
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n}{b_n}=1 \quad a.s.
$$

$$
P(X>x)=1-F(x)=\frac12 \exp \left(-\frac{x^5}\pi\right)
$$

从而 $\lambda>0$ 有

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\left(X_n>\lambda b_n\right) & =\frac12 \exp \left(-\frac{\left(\lambda b_n\right)^5}\pi\right) =\frac12 \exp \left(-\frac{\lambda^5(\pi \ln n)}\pi\right) \\
& =\frac12 \exp \left(-\lambda^5 \ln n\right) = \frac12 n^{-\lambda^5}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step2.** 证明上界 $\le 1$, 只需证对于任意 $\varepsilon>0, \limsup \frac{X_n}{b_n} \le 1 + \varepsilon, a.s.$

令 $\lambda = 1 + \varepsilon$. 考虑事件 $A_n=\left\{X_n>(1+\varepsilon) b_n\right\}$. 有 $P\left(A_n\right)=\frac12 n^{-(1+\varepsilon)^5}$

$$
\sum_{n=1}^\infty P\left(A_n\right)<\infty \implies P\left(A_n, i.o.\right)=0
$$

于是

$$
\limsup _{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n}{b_n} \le 1+\epsilon \quad a.s.
$$

令 $\varepsilon \to 0$ 即知 $\lim \sup \le 1, a.s.$

**Step3.** 证明下界 $\ge 1$,

令 $\lambda=1-\varepsilon > 0$, 考虑事件 $B_n=\left\{X_n>(1-\epsilon) b_n\right\}$.

$$
P\left(B_n\right)=\frac12 n^{-(1-\epsilon)^5} \\
\implies \sum_{n=1}^\infty P\left(B_n\right)=\infty
$$


已知 $\left\{B_n\right\}$ 是相互独立的, 由 Borel-Cantelli第二引理(BC2):  $P\left(B_n i.o.\right)=1$, 所以
$$
\limsup _{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n}{b_n} \ge 1-\epsilon \quad \text { a.s. }
$$

令 $\varepsilon \to 0$, 得 $\lim \sup \ge 1,  a.s.$
